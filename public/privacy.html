DocSwift Privacy Notice

Draft notice — review and update this page before public launch.

Files you upload

PDF processing runs locally in your browser. Files you select are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a DocSwift processing server. Your browser and device still manage the selected files according to their own settings.

Analytics and cookies

This starter does not include Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or a cookie-consent banner. If analytics or other tracking is added, update this notice and provide any consent required by applicable law.

Retention and security

Production deployments should use HTTPS, limit request sizes and processing time, monitor abuse, review server logs and hosting retention, and define a clear deletion/retention policy. No online service can promise absolute security.

Third-party hosting

When deployed, the hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs under its own terms. Replace this draft with the actual service operator's contact details and provider information before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the DocSwift GitHub repository.
